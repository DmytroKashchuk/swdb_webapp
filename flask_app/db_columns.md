# Usefull information about the Spice Works Database

### The SITE_TYPE field (2-char, from legacy Aberdeen field CORPHDQ) indicates the hierarchical role of a site within a company:

- **HQ** – Headquarters
- **UH** – Ultimate Headquarters (top-level/global HQ)
- **Br** – Branch
- **St** – Single (sole location)
- **Ph** – Phantom (non-physical/virtual record)
